## Description (4000 characters max)

```
terminus tells you when to leave for your next class at NUS, and which NUS shuttle bus to catch to get there on time. It works only with the NUS campus shuttles.

Import your NUSMods timetable once. From then on, terminus plans the whole trip: the walk to the stop, the bus's live arrival, the ride, and the walk to your classroom. You get one answer: "Leave by 9:01 · catch the 9:06 R2 at PGP · arrive 9:16, 14 min early".

ONE ANSWER, ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
• The Next bus widget shows when to leave for your next class, on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen. A larger one adds buttons for your favourite places and the buses near you.
• A second widget, Buses near you, shows the next buses at your nearest stop and the one across the road.
• A Live Activity counts down to your bus on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, until you arrive.
• The whole trip at a glance, drawn like a bus's route map: the walk, the bus in its colour, and when you'll get there.
• A heads-up five minutes before you need to set off.
• If you're late, it says so and offers the quickest way there.
• If the bus you'd catch is often busy at that stop and time, it aims for one bus earlier.

IT FOLLOWS YOUR TRIP
• Once your bus leaves, terminus takes it you're on it and shows the next stop and when you'll get there.
• If you miss it, it shows the next way there straight away.
• If you're not going, mark it Not going, on every device.

ANYWHERE ON CAMPUS
• Search any building, stop or room.
• Favourites: pick a stop, building or room and it's saved. The stops your classes use come first.
• Nearby: every bus at the stops around you, live. Wrong side of the road? One tap shows the stop across it.
• Buses: what's coming at any stop, and where a service's buses are along its line.
• The map: every route in its colour on a street map of campus. Pick a service to see its buses moving live; tap a stop for what's coming.

IT LEARNS, AND ASKS FIRST
• Keep missing the bus to one class? It offers to leave one bus earlier for it.
• Skipping a class every week? It offers to stop reminders for it.
Nothing changes unless you say yes, and every choice can be undone.

ALSO
• Today: your classes, when to leave for each, and the trip home.
• In English and Simplified Chinese (简体中文).
• Works with terminus for Android, the menu bar app for Mac and the website. Add an email and they all show the same trip.

YOUR PRIVACY
• No sign-up: the app makes an account of its own. Adding an email is optional, and only needed to use terminus on another device.
• Your location is used to find the stops near you and show where you are on the map, only while you use the app. terminus doesn't keep where you were.
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics SDK.
• Download everything terminus keeps about you from Settings. Delete your account and everything with it from Settings, or clear just your trip history.

terminus is an independent app. It is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with the National University of Singapore. Bus times come from NUS's public shuttle feed.
```

## App Review

Sign-in required: off. Notes:

```
No sign-in needed: a fresh install makes its own account. Setup asks where you live and for a NUSMods timetable link; any public NUSMods share link works, or tap "I'll do this later" (or "Skip setup").

terminus shows live arrival times for the NUS campus shuttle buses in Singapore, in Singapore time. Outside Singapore, location finds no nearby stops: use Search to pick any stop or building (for example "COM3" or "UTown") to see its buses. The shuttles run about 7:00 to 23:00 Singapore time (UTC+8); outside those hours the app says no buses are running.

Location is optional and only used while the app is open. "Add an email" is optional and unlocks nothing extra on the phone.

terminus is an independent student project, not affiliated with the National University of Singapore. Bus times come from NUS's public shuttle feed.
```

## App Privacy

- Privacy policy: https://terminus.run/privacy/policy/
- Privacy choices: https://terminus.run/account/
- Collected, all for App Functionality and none for tracking:
  - Linked to the user: email address, customer support (reports), other user content (timetable, settings, favourites), user ID, device ID (the push token), product interaction (how each trip went; also Product Personalization, for the one-bus-earlier offers).
  - Not linked: crash data.
- Location is not declared. Apple counts data as collected only when it's kept longer than it takes to answer the request; terminus rounds it, uses it for one answer and drops it. (Play's form, which asks differently, lists it as ephemeral.)
