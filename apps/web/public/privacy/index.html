Privacy

Last updated 29 September 2026

terminus is a free, independent student project. It keeps only what it needs to tell you which bus to catch, and you can delete all of it yourself at any time.

What is stored

  • Your email address, to send sign-in links. No password is ever stored.
  • Your timetable: each class's day, time, venue and nearest bus stop, and the NUSMods share link you imported.
  • Your settings: home stops (stop names only, never your address or coordinates), saved places, and your gap and day-hours preferences.
  • Your devices: the name each paired device reported (for example "Google Pixel 8"), when it was added and when it was last used. Sign-in and device tokens are stored only as one-way hashes.

What is not stored

  • Your location. When the app sends your location to find the nearest stop, it is used for that one answer and not saved. Request logs that would contain it are switched off.
  • No tracking, ads, analytics cookies or third-party trackers. The only cookie is the one that keeps you signed in.

To tune its estimates, terminus records anonymous statistics about answers (which stop, which bus, how long until it came). These carry no account, email or location.

Who handles it

Everything runs on Cloudflare (hosting, database, email delivery) in data centres that may be outside Singapore. Bus times come from NUS's shuttle feed; NUS does not receive anything about you. Your data is never sold or shared.

How long it is kept

  • Until you delete your account, which removes everything immediately.
  • Sign-in links expire after 15 minutes, browser sessions after 30 days, and paired devices after 90 days without use.

Your controls

On the account page you can see and edit everything above, download it all as a file, sign out every device, or delete your account.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act: privacy@terminus.rcn.sh.
