Know when to run.

terminus reads your timetable and tells you when to leave for your next class: which shuttle, from which stop, and when walking is faster.

Get it for Android Download for Mac Use it on the web

Free. Android 12 or later, macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon.

How to install
  1. Android: open the downloaded file and allow your browser to install apps when asked. It's installed directly, not from the Play Store, so Play Protect may ask you to confirm.
  2. Mac: open the downloaded disk image and drag terminus to Applications. After that it keeps itself up to date.
  3. Open the app and paste your NUSMods share link. To use one timetable on several devices, sign in with the same email on each, or pair them from your account.
