Know when to run.

terminus reads your timetable and tells you when to leave for your next class: which shuttle, from which stop, and when walking is faster. On your home screen, in your menu bar and on the web.

Get it for Android Download for Mac

Free. Android 12 or later, macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon.

How to install
  1. Android: open the downloaded file and allow your browser to install apps when asked. It's installed directly, not from the Play Store, so Play Protect may ask you to confirm.
  2. Mac: unzip, drag terminus to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open. macOS asks once because the app isn't from the App Store; on macOS 15 and later, allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Sign in at your account, import your NUSMods timetable, and pair each device with the code or QR shown there.

Wherever you look

The same answer on your phone, your Mac and the web, in light or dark.

Follows your timetable

Import from NUSMods once. It knows teaching weeks, recess, exams and public holidays, and sends you home in long gaps.

Leave at the right time

The latest bus that still gets you there, walks along real campus paths at your pace, and a bus earlier when yours is usually packed.

The right side of the road

It picks the stop going your way, even when the one across the road is closer. Or check anywhere with one tap.

Set up in two minutes

Sign in

Email link, no password. Import your NUSMods share link and pick your home stop.

Install

Android widget or Mac menu bar app. Scan the pairing QR code, or type the code.

Glance

That's it. It updates through the day and goes quiet in the evening.

terminus is an independent student project, not affiliated with NUS. Bus times come from NUS's shuttle feed. Walking routes use map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
