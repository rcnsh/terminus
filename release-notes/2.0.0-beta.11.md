### Quietly right

**No more questions.** terminus no longer asks "On the 9:41 D2?" or shows On the bus / Missed it / I'm there buttons. It follows the plan, and with **Notice when I board** on, your phone's location keeps it right: on the bus, missed it, or there.

**One bus, everywhere.** Your phone, widget, notifications, Mac and the web now all name the same bus, and it stays put once it's time to go instead of switching between buses.

**At the stop.** Waiting at the stop, terminus shows the bus you're waiting for ("D2 at 9:41") instead of "Leave by". Riding past other stops, or living next to one, no longer counts as waiting.

**Fixes.** There before your class starts, it says so instead of planning the trip home. A class you missed isn't treated as where you are. Removing a class on the web removes the right one. A class you take off today no longer leaves two trips home.
