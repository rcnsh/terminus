### Your trip, the same on every device

terminus now keeps track of where today's trip is: time to go, on your way, at the stop, on the bus, missed it, or there. Your phone, your Mac and the website all show the same thing, and the buttons under the answer tell it what happened:

- **On the D2**: it follows you onto the bus and shows where to get off and when you'll arrive. Tap it on your phone and your Mac's menu bar changes too.
- **Missed it**: it works out the next way there straight away, and says whether you'll be late.
- **Not going**: that class is dropped for today on every device. **Undo** brings it back.
- **I'm there**: on to whatever's next.

The menu bar on the Mac shows the trip as it happens: `Leave 9:38`, then `D2 9:41`, then `Off 9:52`.

### Today, at a glance

Under the answer, **Today** lists each class with when to leave and how, the trips home, and anything you've skipped.

### Clearer when there's nothing to catch

- **No classes today** says exactly that, and when your next class is. Before, it showed whichever bus was leaving your stop next, which looked like advice. Buses near you are on the **Nearby** tab.
- **Running late for a class?** It stays your answer for 15 minutes after it starts, instead of jumping to your next one while you're still on the bus.
- **After your last class**, it stops telling you to go home once you've had time to get there.
- **In the evening on campus**, it shows the way home instead of the "done for today" moon.
- **Near the end of service** on your way home: *Last D2 from UTown in 18 min*.
