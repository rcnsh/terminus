### Today, your way

**Take something off today.** Swipe an entry in Today to the side on Android, or tap its × on the web and Mac (hover over it on the Mac). It's off today's plan, whether it's from your timetable or one you added, and **Undo** brings it back. Take off the trip home and terminus plans for you staying on campus.

**Directions to the stop.** When terminus says to catch the bus at a stop you don't know, tap **Directions to Museum** in the Android app for walking directions in your maps app.

**The widget opens the app.** Tap anywhere on the widget except its buttons to open terminus on what it's showing. The ↻ button refreshes it.
