### Favourites

**Saved places are now favourites.** No name to type: pick a stop, building or room and it's added, called what it is ("KR MRT", "COM1"). The stops your classes go to are listed first, so the ones you use most are one tap away. Places you named before keep their names, and usual times work as before.

**Clearer answers.** "Library in ~20 min · walking 20 min · or A1 in 14 min" says when, and how long on foot. A packed bus you'd rather not plan around says it may be full.

**Android.** A time picker that matches the app, starting near now for "Go later today". Next and Nearby stay at the front of the buttons, and search results no longer hide under the keyboard. Settings lists your imported classes too, each with Remove. Setup only moves on once there's a home stop or a timetable, and says so when you skip.

**Mac.** Today updates as soon as you take a class off or undo it. The tabs fit a search without cutting off Nearby. The setup and Devices windows close when the Mac is signed out.

**Also.** Error messages read as sentences everywhere, and the account page lays out better on phones.
