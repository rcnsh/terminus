### terminus 2.0

The biggest update since terminus began. What's new since 1.3.10:

**Start in the app. No website, no account.** Install terminus and tap **Get started**. It asks where you live, your NUSMods timetable (share it from NUSMods or paste the link) and how fast you walk, then shows your next bus a minute later. Add an email in Settings when you want your setup on your Mac and the web too; you get a code to sign in, with no password.

**When to leave, not just when the bus comes.** terminus plans each trip to class, home and anywhere else, and keeps one plan: your phone, widget, notifications, Mac and the web all name the same bus. Turn on **Notify me when to leave for class** for a reminder five minutes before you need to go, and again when it's time.

**It follows your trip.** With **Notice when I board** on, the live notification uses your location during a trip to tell when you're on the bus, when you've missed it and when you're there, with nothing to tap. On the bus it lists the stops ahead with live arrival times.

**Today, at a glance.** The rest of your day in one list, with when to leave for each class. Take anything off today with a swipe (or ×), and Undo brings it back. NUS classes end about half an hour early, and terminus goes by that.

**A widget that does more.** Buttons for your timetable, Nearby and your favourites show the quickest way there without opening the app. Tap the rest of the widget to open terminus.

**Favourites.** Pick a stop, building or room and it's saved, called what it is ("KR MRT", "COM1"). Your class stops come first.

**On the Mac.** Set up, sign in with an email code, leave notifications, Today, and your devices with a QR code to add a phone. No more keychain prompts after updates.

**On iPhone.** terminus works as a web app: add it to your Home Screen from Safari.

**Also.** Your phone hears about changes to your trip straight away. terminus learns how long each bus really takes between stops, without keeping anything about you. Clearer answers ("Library in ~20 min · walking 20 min"), directions to stops you don't know, error messages that read as sentences, and Clear trip history in Settings.
