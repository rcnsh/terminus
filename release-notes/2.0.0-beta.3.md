### "On the 9:41 D2?"

When your bus leaves, the notification that told you when to go asks one question, with three buttons: **On it**, **Missed it** and **Not going**. It asks once, in the notification that's already there, never in a new one. The large widget has the same buttons.

- **On it**: your arrival now comes from that bus's live position, not an estimate: *Off at COM3 9:52 · 5 min early*.
- **Missed it**: the next way there, in the same notification, and whether you'll be late.
- **Not going**: that class is dropped for today on every device.
- **No answer** counts as "on it". Most people catch the bus they were told to, so ignoring the question is fine.

### Your phone hears about changes

On Android phones with Google Play services, terminus tells the app when your trip changes (time to go, the bus leaving, a missed bus) instead of the app checking on a timer. Answer on your phone and your other phones change straight away. Phones without Play services work as before.

The **live notification** now only runs during a trip, from time to go until you're there, so it no longer uses battery all day.

### It learns, and asks first

- Missed the bus to the same class three times this month? It offers to **leave one bus earlier** for it.
- Skipped a class three weeks running? It offers to **stop reminders** for it.
- Never answer the question? After five trips it **stops asking**. You can turn it back on in Settings.

Nothing changes unless you say yes, and Settings lists what you chose, each with **Undo**. terminus keeps one word per class trip per day for 35 days to do this; the privacy page has the details.

### Also

- Deleting your account now deletes today's trip at once, instead of at midnight.
