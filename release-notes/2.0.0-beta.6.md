### Follow the ride

Once you tap **On it**, the notification follows the bus: the next stop, how many are left, and a countdown to getting off at yours. On Android 16 it's a progress bar with a point at each stop. On Android 16 QPR2 and later it's also a Live Update, pinned at the top with a chip in the status bar ("Off 9:52"). The large widget shows the same ride.

Where the bus is comes from the time it left and when it gets you there (live when terminus knows which bus you're on), so the notification moves on stop by stop.

### Shortcuts

Long-press the terminus icon for **Next**, your top two saved places and **Nearby**.

### Also

- The widget shows the trip's buttons while you're on your way, and says what to do when you've missed the bus.
- Today shows the bus you're on ("On the D2 · off at UTown · arrive 9:52") instead of a leave-by time that has passed. This is on Android and the iPhone web app.
- iPhone web app: without a connection it now shows the trip as you last saw it, including a bus you just said you were on.
