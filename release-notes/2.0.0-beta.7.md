### The Mac catches up

**When to leave.** Turn on **Notify me when to leave for class** in Settings. terminus then tells you five minutes before you need to leave, and again when it's time. When the bus leaves it asks whether you're on it, with **On it**, **Missed it** and **Not going** as buttons (hover over the notification, or pick the Alerts style in System Settings to always see them).

**Today.** The menu shows the rest of your day: when to leave for each class, or the bus you're on.

**Set up on the Mac.** A new account can now be set up without the website: where you live, your NUSMods timetable, how fast you walk, then notifications and location. It's under Settings → **Set up…** any time.

**Devices.** Settings → **Devices…** lists everything signed in to your account, lets you remove one, and adds a new one with a code or a QR code to scan with your phone.
