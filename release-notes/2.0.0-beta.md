### Start in the app. No website, no account.

Install terminus on Android and tap **Get started**. It asks three things (where you live, your NUSMods timetable, how fast you walk) and shows your next bus a minute later. No email and no password. Nothing to pair.

- **Where do you live?** Pick your hall or residence and its stops fill themselves in, or pick a stop if you live off campus.
- **Your timetable.** In NUSMods, tap **Share** and choose terminus, or paste the link. It shows what it found, including any rooms it couldn't place.
- **Everything else, in Settings.** Home stops, the hours of your day, gaps long enough to go home in, walking pace, packed buses, saved places and classes added by hand. The website becomes optional.

### Add an email when you want to

**Settings → Add your email** keeps your setup if you lose your phone and lets you use it on your Mac and the web. We email you a code; type it into the app. Reading your mail on another device? Open the link in the email and choose the number your app is showing.

Already have an account? **I have an account: sign in** on a fresh install, and you're straight back to your timetable.

### Sign in on the Mac with an email code

The Mac app signs in the same way: type your email, then the code. Pairing codes still work too.

### Your devices, from your phone

**Settings → Devices** lists every phone and Mac on your account, and removes any of them. **Add a device** shows a code for your Mac and a QR code for another phone. You get an email whenever a device is added or removed.

### Sign-in emails that arrive

Every sign-in email now leads with a code, and the code is in the subject line too. University spam filters hold back emails that contain only a link.
