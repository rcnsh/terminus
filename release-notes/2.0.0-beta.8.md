### Fewer taps, more places

**terminus notices your trip.** Turn on **Notice when I board** in the Android app. During a trip, the live notification uses your location to tell when you're on the bus, when you've missed it, and when you're there, so you don't have to tap anything. While it's following the trip, nothing asks you "On the 9:41 D2?". If it gets something wrong, tap **Not right?** on the card. It only uses your location during a trip, and keeps what it means, never where you were.

**The widget does more.** A row of buttons under the answer: **Timetable**, **Nearby**, and the places you go most. Tap one and the widget shows the quickest way there from where you are, without opening the app. It goes back to your timetable by itself.

**More than classes.**
- Give a saved place a usual time (the gym on Tuesdays at 6 pm) in Settings, and it's planned like a class.
- On a place, **Go later today at…** plans a one-off trip.
- **Not on campus today** takes every trip off today's plan, and **Back on campus** puts them back.

**Learning how long buses take.** Rides terminus sees from start to end teach it how long each bus really takes between stops. Nothing about you is kept with them.
