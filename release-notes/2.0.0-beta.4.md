### Clear trip history

To notice a class you keep missing or skipping, terminus remembers one word per class trip per day for 35 days. You can now forget all of it at once, without deleting your account: **Settings → Trip history → Clear trip history** in the app, or the same button on the [account page](https://terminus.rcn.sh/account).

Choices you've already made for a class (one bus earlier, no reminders) stay, each with its own **Undo**. If terminus had stopped asking "On the 9:41 D2?", clearing the history turns the question back on.

### Getting ready for Google Play

- **No more "Allow all the time" location.** terminus only uses your location while it's open. In the background the widget follows your timetable and your trip, as it already did for most people.
- **Alarms & reminders.** For the heads-up to come exactly on time, Android now asks you to allow terminus to set alarms. If it's off, a note under the notification switches takes you to the setting. Without it the heads-up can come a few minutes late.
