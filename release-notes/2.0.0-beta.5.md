### No more keychain prompt on the Mac after every update

Until now, the first launch after each update asked you to allow terminus into your keychain. macOS knows terminus by its code, which changes with every version. The Mac app now keeps its sign-in in a file only your user account can read (`~/Library/Application Support/terminus/device-token`) instead.

**This update asks one last time**, while it moves your sign-in out of the keychain. Click **Allow** (or **Always Allow**) and you won't see it again.

### "On the 9:41 D2?" now actually asks

The question when your bus leaves was hardly ever asked. Your phone and widget kept replacing the bus you'd been told to catch with a later one, so the bus it was waiting on never left. Now the bus is fixed from your leave-by time: the question comes when it leaves, and **On it** follows that bus. While you're on the bus it says the stop you get off at ("Off at UTown"), not the class.

### terminus on iPhone

Open [terminus.rcn.sh/app](https://terminus.rcn.sh/app/) in Safari and choose **Add to Home Screen**. You get the answer card, Today, your saved places, Nearby, and notifications for when to leave and "did you catch it?" (iOS 16.4 or later). It also works without a connection, showing the last times it saw. The same page can be installed in Chrome on Android too.
