### terminus 3.10.0

**New:** while something loads, every screen and widget shows its shape instead of "Checking…". Lines and stops open at once from what's already on your device. At night, a pinned stop lists its services and when each starts again. Timetable guesses for shuttles use how often each one really runs at that hour.

**Fixes:** "Earlier bus" and "Later bus" stay chosen: on a day with no class, for the trip home, and when the app refreshes in the background. For a class days away, they offer buses on its own day. The pinned card no longer leaves a gap under it at night.
