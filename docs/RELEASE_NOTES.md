### terminus 3.11.0

**New:** terminus is on Apple Watch. It shows the next bus and when to leave, the stops near you, and complications for your watch face, and you sign in from Settings › Devices on your iPhone. A trip you follow also shows in the watch's Smart Stack. On a tablet, an unfolded phone or a wide window, every app puts things side by side: the card beside Today, a stop beside the list, and a Settings page beside its menu. A phone held on its side shows the answer first. On the map, the line buttons sit together in one bar across the top. Devices shows a picture of each device: a phone, a Mac or a watch.

**Fixes:** on iPhone and iPad the map fills the whole screen. Stop names no longer cover the stop next to them. The pinned card is as tall as the stop it shows, without a gap under it.
