# Nearby is a tab of its own, between Now and Stops

Nearby was a chip on Now, beside Next and the favourites (ADR 0014). Every other chip there is somewhere to go, and the card under it answers how to get there and when to leave; Nearby is where you are, and swapped the card for boards. It was the odd one in the row and in the code: a skeleton of its own, a refresh of its own, and "Is this wrong?" switched off for it. Now is the page for the day's plan, and Nearby didn't belong to it. So Nearby is a tab: Now, Nearby, Stops, Map, Settings, in the phone app, the Android app and the web app. Its page is the stops around you as their signs, under its title in a band of sky, as Stops has; Now's chips are only places to go.

What Nearby shows and does is as ADR 0014 left it: up to three stops at once, a stop's name opening its board and a row its service's line, both in the Stops tab, and the star pinning the stop there. Stops keeps its one job too, any other stop, and its home is unchanged. In a wide window Nearby stays in the centred 560 column: it has no pages of its own to put beside it, since what it opens is the Stops tab's (ADR 0015). It refreshes at the card's pace and only while it's the tab on screen, so it asks the server no more than the chip did (rule 2), and it asks for the location when it opens, as the chip did when tapped.

The server is unchanged: `/api/me/nearby` and the answer's `nearby` mode stay, so the widgets, the watch and older versions of the apps work as they did. The watch already has Nearby as a page beside Now (ADR 0016), and the widgets show it in place. The Mac's popover has no tab bar: its chips are its navigation, so Nearby stays a chip there. `terminus://nearby` and Android's app shortcut open the tab.

We considered putting Nearby at the top of the Stops tab, above Pinned, with four tabs as before. Stacked as it is, it pushed the pinned stops two screens down, which costs most for the stops pinned because they aren't nearby; paged one sign at a time, as Pinned is, it lost the three stops at once that ADR 0014 names as what Nearby does best. A fifth tab costs a place in the bar, and five still fit a phone's width.
