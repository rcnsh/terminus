# Android refreshes on alarms at the server's moments, and the app polls at a fixed pace

The Android widgets and the leave alerts have to stay true with no process running, through Doze, on a phone that gives the background no location. A bus that has left must not sit bright on the home screen, and a leave alert must fire on time. So the widget runs on one chain of alarms (`widget/Refresher.kt`), and leave alerts (`LeaveAlerts.kt`) ride on it, so the chain also runs with no widget while alerts or the live notification are on.

- **Network refreshes at the server's moments.** The answer says when it changes: the card's `nextChangeAt` (the bus leaves, the trip's next phase, going stale) and the plan's `refreshAt`. The refresh is an idle-safe alarm (`setWhileIdle`), never sooner than 15 s after the last (`MIN_GAP_MS`), or 60 s with no moment from the server. Doze lets such an alarm through at most every ~9 minutes. Without "Alarms & reminders" allowed it's inexact and can run a few minutes late, which is why the widget shows clock times: they stay true until then. The server leaves a late bus's sliding leave-by out of `nextChangeAt` until it's 30 s ahead, so fetching at it doesn't ask every few seconds.
- **The alarm fetches there and then.** The broadcast keeps itself open (`finishAsync`, `goAsync`) and asks for `/api/me/next` alone; the offline day plan and the widgets showing a place or Nearby follow in a job once the lock is free (`extras`). The alarm's idle allowance is a few seconds, and a queued job can wait hours once Android's quota for prompt jobs runs out. Only with no network does it queue a job that waits for one, and it redraws meanwhile, so a stale answer dims (`redrawWhileWaiting`).
- **Redraws without the network** at the moments the widget changes by itself: "Leave by" turning into "Leave now", the next stop on a ride, an answer going stale, the hour's sky changing, the offline line moving on through the day plan (`armRedraw`, `redrawAt`). These don't wake the phone. Only the refresh for alerts and the live notification does (`refreshAlarms`, `notifying`).
- **An hourly floor job** in case an alarm is missed. It leaves the network alone while the answer is fresh and the next alarm is armed (`onTrack`).
- **After a failure** it tries again through the alarm, 1, 2, 4, 8, then every 15 minutes (`retryDelay`), never before a 429's or 503's wait (`Quiet`). Waiting for the floor instead would leave the widget and the alerts quiet for as long as Doze stretches it.
- **After a reboot, an app update, or a clock, timezone or locale change,** the alarms are armed again and the cached answer's leave alert with them (`RefreshReceiver`), since alarms are gone and the widget's text was formatted before the change.
- **The background has no location.** The app doesn't ask for "Allow all the time", so a background refresh sends the last fix the app had, or none, and the server follows the timetable. A widget button that needs where you are (Nearby, a place) starts a short foreground service with the location type (`WidgetModeService`), which Android allows when a widget tap starts it.
- **Nearby's minute counts need exact alarms.** An inexact alarm can run as late again as it is away, so a count would stand a minute or more behind; without exact alarms the widget shows clock times alone (`NearbyLayouts.minutes`). Inexact redraws step a third of the way at a time until within 25 s (`stepTo`). Turning off "Alarms & reminders" cancels exact alarms and stops the app with no broadcast, so an inexact backstop a little behind each exact redraw turns the counts back into clock times (`backstopAt`). Counting a minute down only redraws; the shared board is fetched again only when its times turn old or a bus shown goes (`boardRefetchAt`).

The app, while a tab is on screen and the app is in front, polls at fixed paces set by the API's caches: Now and Nearby every 30 s (`POLL_MS`), a Stops page every 15 s, the map's live buses every 5 s and an open stop's board every 15 s, the list of lines once a minute. The countdown ticks on the phone in between. Now doesn't follow the card's `nextChangeAt` or the plan's `refreshAt`: a leave time that keeps sliding (a late bus) moves them a few seconds on with every answer, so following them would poll every 5 s. The Mac and the server's trip engine wait 30 s too.

We considered delayed WorkManager jobs for the refresh, which Doze defers for hours; one periodic job, which leaves a departed bus bright until it runs; and background location, which would ask more of the user than an answer to "which bus" is worth.
