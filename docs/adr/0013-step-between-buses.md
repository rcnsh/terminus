# Classes end 30 minutes early for everyone; a step to a later bus covers the rest

A one-hour tutorial usually lets out about ten minutes early, at :50, but `ENDS_EARLY_MIN` treats every NUSMods class as ending 30 minutes before its timetabled end, so the trip home shows while you're still in the room. We considered a different figure for each kind of lesson and rejected it: any number is a guess that's wrong for some class on some day. Instead `ENDS_EARLY_MIN` stays 30 for every class, meaning "show the next trip from here", and the card lets you step to the bus before or after the one it picked ("Earlier bus", "Later bus", step.ts). The server still works out every bus and keeps the choice per trip for the day, so the clients only send it back. "Later" goes up to 20 minutes late for a class, or an hour after the best bus on any other trip. Steps are counted by direction and kind of trip, with no account on them (analytics.md), so a later change to the 30 minutes can rest on evidence. They aren't outcomes: `trip_outcomes` checks its kinds, and adding one would mean rebuilding the table.
