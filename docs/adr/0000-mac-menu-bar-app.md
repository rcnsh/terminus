# The Mac app lives in the menu bar and refreshes on its own clock, quietly

The Mac app is a menu bar item (`LSUIElement`): no Dock icon, no menus, not in ⌘-Tab. A window of its own (Setup, Settings, the map, Sparkle's) is then lost once another app's covers it, so while one is open the app becomes an ordinary one, with a Dock icon, ⌘-Tab and its menus, and goes back to the menu bar alone when the last one closes (`DockIcon`). A window that opens without becoming key counts too, so occlusion changes are watched as well as key changes, and a close is judged once the window has gone. While the app is hidden (⌘H) the policy is left alone: dropping the Dock icon then would leave no way back to the windows. We rejected staying an ordinary app all the time (a Dock icon for something that lives in the menu bar) and staying a menu bar item all the time (windows lost behind others).

The menu bar's text is recomputed from the answer's times on the model's own 30-second clock (`AppModel.clock`), set only when the text or the dimming would change, since every assignment redraws the item. A `TimelineView` in the menu bar label starves app launch, so it isn't used there. The label is one `Text` with the symbol inline: a `Label` gets the status button's wide gap between image and title. Inside the popover, `Ticking` redraws every second only while the popover is open; its views stay alive while it's closed, and a countdown nobody sees would redraw all day.

The popover never resizes on a tab switch. The menu bar window grows to fit taller content with its top edge fixed, but shrinks around its middle and then slides back up under the menu bar, so the content is never allowed to get shorter than the window (`floor`), and `Popover.fit` shrinks the window itself, top edge pinned, after SwiftUI's update rather than inside it. Content taller than the screen scrolls inside the popover with the footer in view; content that fits doesn't scroll, as a scroll view resized on every switch shakes and flickers its bar. The `ScrollView` stays in the tree either way, so a search being typed keeps its state as it grows.

Refreshes keep to what the API can show: 30 s while the popover is open, 5 minutes otherwise, 10 while resting, twice those in Low Power Mode, and never under 30 s (60 in Low Power), the same as the server's trip engine, since a leave-by that keeps sliding would otherwise bring the loop down to seconds; the API caches each stop for 15 s, so faster shows nothing new (`AppModel.nextDelay`). A bus leaving is checked 31 s after it leaves. After a failure the loop retries at 5, 15 and 45 s, since Wi-Fi is often not up right after a wake, and never sooner than a `Retry-After`. Nothing refreshes while the Mac sleeps, its screens sleep or it's locked; the three are kept apart, the lock is asked about on wake rather than assumed (its notice may come before the sleep, or not at all), and the several notices a wake brings end in one refresh. With the popover closed a fix up to 10 minutes old is reused, and none is asked for while resting, since asking CoreLocation every two minutes all day costs battery and blinks the location arrow. The leave reminders are local notifications scheduled ahead from each refresh, so they fire on time while the loop waits.

Sparkle installs a downloaded update only once the popover and every window are closed (`Updater.blocksInstall`: any window at the normal level, whatever its scene, so a new window counts without a list to keep; not the menu bar item's window, nor the debug build's `popover` window), since relaunching would close a window from under the user. Sparkle is required at 2.10.0 or later, past the fixes for CVE-2025-10015 and CVE-2025-10016, and `build.sh` builds with `--force-resolved-versions`, so a newer Sparkle never arrives unreviewed.
