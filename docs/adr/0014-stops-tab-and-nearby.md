# Nearby shows the stops around you; the Stops tab shows any other stop

Nearby (a chip on Now) and the second tab both showed the boards of the stops around you, and Nearby did it better: three stops at once, one tap from the card. People who used Nearby had no reason to open the tab, so they missed what only it has: pinned stops anywhere on campus, a search for any stop or service, and a service's whole line. So each has one job. Nearby is the stops around you; its signs open a stop's board or a service's line, a star on each pins it, and a row under them leads to the tab. The tab's home is the pinned stops, each with its first three rows, then every service; it no longer opens on the nearest stop. It's called Stops (车站), after what fills it, rather than Buses, which every tab is about. We considered putting the pinned stops in Nearby too and leaving the tab only the search and the services, and dropping the tab altogether (Now, Map, Settings), with stops and services found from Nearby, the search and the map; both were rejected for now as larger changes than the overlap needed. The code, the string ids and the addresses keep the name `buses` (`buses.js`, `#buses/stop/<code>`, `tab_buses`, `BusesView`), so links and saved addresses still work and the change stays small.
