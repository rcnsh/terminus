### terminus 2.6

A big one: everything since 2.4.2. The highlights are below; every change is listed further down.

**A Buses tab.** Every stop and every service, not just the ones for your classes, with services that have stopped for the day greyed out. Pull down on Now or Buses to refresh, and your bus pulls in.

**Trips that change buses.** Where no single bus goes there, terminus plans a change: the card shows both buses, follows you onto the second, and has you wait at the change until it leaves.

**The map.** Tap a bus to see where it's going and the stops ahead; tap a stop for its board. Stop and bus sheets look alike, a bus's stops open their own, and tapping **Map** again goes back to the whole campus. Place names show in Chinese too.

**On Android,** the widget is redrawn under Now's sky, with a layout for each size, and an **×** takes a row off Today, with Undo in its place. Favourites fold in and out everywhere.

**Is this wrong?** opens a sheet with quick reasons, and lets you suggest the stop you actually use for a building.

**NUS's own days off,** starting with Well-Being Day, count as holidays.

**A new address,** terminus.run. The apps keep using terminus.rcn.sh, which NUS Wi-Fi lets through, so nothing changes for you.

**Under the hood:** easier to use with TalkBack, VoiceOver and large text, readable colours on every sky, steadier when the network or NUS's feed is down, and a "~" on every time that's a guess rather than live.
