### terminus 3.9.0

**New:** Nearby is a departure board. Each stop has a row per line saying where it's going, with the next bus large and the two after it written down beside it; a timetable guess is marked "~". On the Stops tab, your pinned stops are Nearby's own card, one at a time: swipe or tap a stop's name to switch. Below them, every line says up to three stops it passes and how many of its buses are out. Stops are named as on their signs everywhere.

**Fixes:** "Later bus" is never the bus already on the card, and it stays there past the leave-by until you've left. Live times keep working through a short NUS sign-in outage. On the iPhone, answering a notification no longer crashes the app. On the Mac, the popover no longer jumps when switching tabs, and the map and Settings show in the Dock and ⌘-Tab while open.
