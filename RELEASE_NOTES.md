### terminus 3.4.3

**Fixes:** signing in from a guest account, or into a different account, starts afresh: the last account's answers and widget choices don't carry over. Settings save only what you changed, so edits made elsewhere or during a timetable import aren't lost. On Android, a trip that changes buses shows the second bus on the card and the widgets, and a stop and a building with the same code are two results in the pickers. On the Mac, a weekly class's heads-up comes every week.
