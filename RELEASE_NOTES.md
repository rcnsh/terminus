### terminus 3.5.0

**New:** on Android, the Buses near you widget shows your stop and the one across the road side by side, so the side a bus is on says which way it goes, and says how many minutes until each bus. Setup and Settings ask for Alarms & reminders, which keeps those minutes on time. New to NUS, a switch in Settings › Appearance, has the widget say where each bus goes and the places it passes.

**Fixes:** a dismissed leave reminder on Android stays dismissed, even while the bus feed is down, and stops waking the phone for that trip. Bus times count from the feed's own clock, so they no longer run a little early. Standing in a building, walks follow its paths, so a lecture theatre no longer suggests walking somewhere far. A class after a service has closed for the day is planned for its last buses, and the card's "packed" belongs to the bus it names.
